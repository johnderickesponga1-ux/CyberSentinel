HOW TO PLAY

🚶 Walk with arrow keys / WASD, hold the D-pad, or tap the map next to your character. A (Enter/Space) talks and advances text, B (Esc) goes back.
🌿 Tall grass hides wild problems (attacks and alerts). Send out FIREWALRUS and pick the right defensive code.
🏢 Buildings are problem systems (the hacked machines). Battle them: your tool-mons' moves run commands that reveal parts (ports, IPs, passwords, flags). Throw the right part at the system's weakness to deal damage.
➕ The Patch Center heals your team. 🔴 Capsules on the map hold PATCH POTIONS to heal in battle.

    DEFENDER MODE (BLUE TEAM)

    You protect the school network. Alerts arrive one at a time. Choose the right response before the timer runs out. Some alerts are real attacks and some are normal activity. Wrong calls cost health. Survive every alert to win.

    TOURNAMENT: YOU VS THE SYSTEM

    A 1v1 bracket against the system's AI. Each round the system attacks (pick the right DEFENSE in time), then you attack (it tries to block). At 0% health you lose. The system learns which attacks you struggle with and uses them more. Beat 3 opponents to become champion.
